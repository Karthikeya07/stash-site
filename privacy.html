Unspent Privacy Policy

Last updated November 1, 2026

In short: Unspent doesn't collect any data. Your gift cards, PINs and photos stay on your own devices. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking.

What Unspent stores, and where

  • Gift cards (store, card number, barcode, balance, nickname, expiry date, and an optional photo of the front) are stored only on your iPhone. They aren't included in iCloud Backup.
  • PINs are stored in your iPhone's Keychain and never leave this iPhone, unless you turn on "Show PINs on Apple Watch". If you do, they're sent directly to your paired Apple Watch and kept in its Keychain.
  • Apple Watch gets a copy of your cards from your iPhone over Apple's encrypted connection between paired devices.
  • The Lock Screen widget sees only your total balance and the number of cards.

The developer of Unspent never receives any of this. Unspent has no server.

Location

If you allow it, Unspent uses your location to show which of your gift cards can be used at stores nearby. It sends Apple Maps a store name (such as "Target") and your approximate area to find nearby stores. Card numbers and balances are never sent.

If you turn on "Remind me at stores", Unspent asks for location access "Always" so it can notify you when you arrive at one of those stores, even when the app is closed. This is optional; Unspent works fully without location access.

Camera and photos

The camera is used only to scan a gift card's barcode, number and PIN, and the scan is processed on your iPhone. If you pick a photo instead, only that photo is read. Nothing is uploaded.

Encrypted backups

You can export an encrypted backup file protected by a password you choose. You decide where it's saved or sent. Unspent can't read it without your password, and neither can anyone else.

Deleting your data

Delete any card from its screen, or delete everything in Settings → Delete all cards. Deleting the app also removes your cards. PINs left in the Keychain are cleared the next time Unspent is installed.

Children

Unspent doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
